#+TITLE: My Personal Hacker Space

The site `venikx.com` is my personal homepage, it serves as a very
visually pleasing portfolio, a hackerspace where I make digital art
experiments, curated blog posts if I want to dive deeper into topics,
and a references map to all my personal notes.

- most of the blog posts and personal notes are written in org-mode,
  some blog posts are written with mdx if I need more visually
  engaging or interactive examples
- my personal notes are currently a mix between org-roam and
  denote-org, as I'm in the middle of switching to denote-org
- I build the digital art experiences purely in astro pages, as most
  of them revolve around working with canvas

Think of it as my own personal hackerspace, that looks super cool to
show-off to other people that describes my life's work if they find it
interesting to dig deeper.
